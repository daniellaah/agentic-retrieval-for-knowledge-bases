# Why Accuracy Can Reward Guessing

## Core idea

A scoring rule that rewards correct answers but gives the same credit to a wrong answer and an abstention can encourage uncertain guessing. OpenAI describes how training and evaluation incentives help plausible false statements persist. Unavailable or arbitrary facts cannot reliably be recovered from language patterns alone.

## Practical implication

Distinguish correct answers, unsupported errors, and appropriate uncertainty in evaluation. A system should not gain an advantage merely by making confident guesses on questions it cannot answer.
